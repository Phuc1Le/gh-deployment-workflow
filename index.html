About

I like figuring out how things work well enough to rebuild them better. That curiosity is what pulled me toward computer science and mathematics — fields where an idea only counts once it runs, and where the difference between a clever solution and a reliable one is usually the harder half of the work.

My current research at HeLab focuses on scGPT brain cell-type annotation, from fine-tuning to embedding-based reference mapping. Most of what I build sits at the intersection of AI systems and the infrastructure that keeps them dependable: data pipelines, APIs, and cloud services that hold up outside of a demo. I care less about a technology being new and more about whether it is the right tool for the problem in front of me, which keeps me reading documentation and picking up new tools on a fairly regular basis.

Outside of coursework and projects, I'm usually on a field or in a pool, and I try to stay involved in campus life through the leadership and student-support roles I take on at Rochester.
